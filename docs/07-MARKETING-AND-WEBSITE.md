## 1. Positioning & Value Proposition

### Core Audience

Independent commercial janitorial and facility-cleaning owner-operators (typically 5–50 employees) who conduct walkthroughs and submit customized bids, but lack dedicated sales staff to perform disciplined, multi-touch follow-up on dormant proposals.

### Core Problem

Commercial cleaning owners invest substantial time conducting facility walkthroughs, calculating labor, and preparing custom proposals. Once proposals are sent, operational fires (crew call-offs, supply issues, customer inspections) take priority. Follow-up drops off after 1–2 attempts. Unresolved proposals remain in limbo—neither won nor lost—leaving cash and future rebid dates buried in silence.

### One-Sentence Proposition

> **JanSan Sweep follows up on dormant commercial-cleaning proposals and turns silence into a documented decision, conversation, revision request, correct contact, or future rebid date.**

### Category Definition

- **Category:** Productized B2B sales-support sprint & proposal reactivation.
- **Delivery Model:** Founder-led, transparent human phone and email outreach.
- **Not:** Cold lead generation, purchased marketing lists, an AI voice bot, autodialer software, or debt collection.

## 2. Brand Voice & Copy Guardrails

### Brand Voice Attributes

- **Direct & Pragmatic:** Speaks peer-to-peer to blue-collar and facility service business owners. No corporate jargon or marketing fluff.
- **Transparent & Accountable:** Explicit about what is promised (disciplined human execution and clear data) and what is not (guaranteed closed contracts or revenue).
- **Calm & Respectful:** No aggressive sales pressure, artificial scarcity, or sensationalized claims.
- **Process-Oriented:** Frames follow-up as operational discipline rather than sales magic.

### Prohibited Language (Strict Compliance Rule)

Do **NOT** use any of the following phrases or concepts in website copy, marketing emails, or metadata:

| Prohibited Phrase / Concept | Why It Is Banned | Compliant Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| "Guaranteed contracts / revenue / ROI" | We do not control prospect decisions or client bidding. | "Documented outcomes and clear pipeline status" |
| "Cold leads" or "We find new clients" | JanSan Sweep only works existing, client-generated proposals. | "Follow up on your past walkthroughs and sent proposals" |
| "AI voice agents / automated outreach" | All outreach is manually conducted by a human (Clay). | "Disciplined, manual human phone calls and personalized emails" |
| "Explode your pipeline / 7-figure agency" | Hyped marketing agency clichés destroy trust with owners. | "Clear the ambiguity from your proposal backlog" |
| "Only 2 spots left today!" (artificial urgency) | Manipulative and untruthful. | "Founding sprint limited to the first 3 clients at $199" |
| "Pay only if you close" / pure commission | Closing depends on client pricing, capacity, and the buyer. | "Prepaid sprint covering dedicated execution and outcome tracking" |

### Section 3: The Problem — The Commercial Proposal Graveyard

**Section Header:** The Walkthrough Was Weeks Ago. What Happened to the Bid?

**Body Copy:** Running an independent commercial cleaning business means fighting daily operational fires:

- Cleaners call off at 4:30 PM.
- A supply order didn't arrive.
- A property manager demands an immediate walk-through inspection.

Meanwhile, legitimate commercial proposals you labored over sit untouched in your inbox, spreadsheet, or bidding software. You called once or twice. They didn't pick up. Now weeks have passed, and nobody owns the next touch.

**The Three Hidden Costs of Dormant Bids:**

1. **Forgotten Pipeline:** Prospects get busy. Many meant to award the work or need a simple scope adjustment, but got distracted.
2. **Lost Rebid Timing:** If a competitor won, that contract will come up for renewal in 12 months. Without follow-up, you will never know the rebid date.
3. **False Pipeline Hope:** Keeping dead bids on your mental ledger wastes attention. A definitive "no" cleans your board so you focus on live revenue.

5. **Step 5: Outcome Dashboard & Closeout** — At the end of Day 7, you receive a clean disposition dashboard showing the status of every single proposal—including future renewal dates, confirmed losses, and recommended 30/90-day next steps.

### Section 8: The Outcome Dashboard — What Happens to Your 20 Records

**Section Header:** Silence Turned Into 12 Documented Dispositions.

**Subhead:** Every proposal you hand us receives one of twelve clean outcomes:

1. **Review Requested (RR):** Prospect wants a phone call or meeting to revisit the bid.
2. **Revision Requested (RV):** Prospect wants scope, frequency, or pricing updated.
3. **Open Pending (OP):** Project is still active with a firm future decision date.
4. **Rebid Date (RB):** Competitor or incumbent won; renewal month identified for next year.
5. **Won Independently (WI):** Prospect already awarded the contract; records updated.
6. **Lost — Competitor / Incumbent (LI):** Prospect retained current cleaner or chose another vendor.
7. **Lost — Price (LP):** Prospect indicated budget or price was the deciding factor.
8. **Lost — Timing / Budget (LT):** Facility project postponed or budget unavailable.
9. **No Response (NR):** Full multi-touch sequence completed with no reply.
10. **Bad Data (BD):** Facility closed, contact left, or invalid contact details.
11. **Do Not Contact (DNC):** Recipient requested no further contact (suppressed immediately).
12. **Ineligible (X):** Record did not meet commercial proposal standards.

### Section 9: Frequently Asked Questions

#### 1. Why shouldn't I just have my office manager or myself do this?

You can, and if you have the hours to consistently call, email, track notes, and categorize 20 older bids while managing cleaners, inspections, and payroll, you should. JanSan Sweep exists for owners who recognize that while they *can* do it, it consistently slips down their priority list.

#### 2. Aren't proposals from 6 months ago completely dead?

Some are. Finding out they are dead is valuable—it clears false pipeline off your plate. But in commercial cleaning, contracts frequently drag out: managers get sidetracked, incumbents slip in quality after 90 days, or budgets unlock in a new quarter. More importantly, when an incumbent was retained, we uncover the exact month that contract comes up for bid again.

#### 3. Do you guarantee signed cleaning contracts?

No. Anyone promising guaranteed signed commercial cleaning contracts is either overpromising or misrepresenting what they do. We do not control your bid pricing, your operational capacity, or the facility manager's budget. We guarantee disciplined, high-caliber human execution, transparent communication, and complete record reporting.

#### 4. Why won't you work purely on commission?

Closing a commercial cleaning deal depends on your labor rates, square-foot pricing, walkthrough credibility, references, and client negotiation—none of which JanSan Sweep controls. We deliver dedicated labor, research, calling, and reporting. You pay for the execution of a professional sprint, just as you pay an estimator or inspector for their work.

#### 5. How do you introduce yourself to my prospects?

We are completely transparent. We say: *"Hi [Name], this is Clay with JanSan Sweep following up on behalf of [Your Company] regarding the cleaning proposal for [Facility] sent back in [Month]..."* We never pretend to be your full-time internal employee, nor do we misrepresent our role. Facility managers appreciate the honest, professional follow-up.

#### 6. What kind of records qualify for the sprint?

Records must be commercial cleaning opportunities (offices, medical clinics, industrial facilities, churches, schools, dealerships) where the prospect previously requested a quote, invited you to a walkthrough, or received a proposal. We do not accept residential cleaning leads, purchased cold lists, or government opportunities under sealed-bid communication blackouts.

#### 7. What happens when a prospect says they want to talk?

We do not attempt to price jobs or negotiate terms. We immediately confirm the best time and contact info, notify you via email/phone within minutes, and hand the opportunity back to you with detailed conversation notes so you can close it.

#### 8. Is this an automated AI dialer or robocalling service?

No. Every phone call is manually dialed by a human being. Every voicemail and email is personalized. We do not use soundboard bots, ringless voicemails, autodialers, or automated mass blasting.
