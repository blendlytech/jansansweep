# JanSan Sweep — Client Delivery SOP

## Stage 1: Sale

- Confirm fit
- Send one-page scope and service agreement
- Collect payment
- Schedule intake
- Explain data eligibility and security before accepting files

## Stage 2: Intake

Request these fields for every record:

- Opportunity ID
- Prospect business
- Contact name and role
- Business phone
- Business email
- Facility/site
- Proposal date
- Proposed scope
- Approximate value (optional)
- Last contact date
- Last-known status
- Relationship context
- Client salesperson/estimator
- Restrictions
- Evidence or context showing prior proposal engagement

## Stage 3: Validation

- Remove duplicates
- Exclude consumers and purchased cold contacts
- Exclude records without legitimate prior engagement
- Flag government and formal procurement opportunities
- Flag records older than 12 months for client approval
- Correct obvious data formatting errors
- Confirm the handoff contact and response expectation
- Obtain written approval of final records and scripts

## Stage 4: Prioritization

1. Recent proposals with no first follow-up
2. High-value proposals with known decision-makers
3. Prospects that engaged and then delayed
4. Lost-to-incumbent records likely to have renewals
5. Older records with strong context
6. Incomplete records last

## Stage 5: Baseline Outreach Sequence

- Day 1: Manual call; personalized email if appropriate
- Day 3: Manual call at a different time; concise voicemail where appropriate
- Day 5: Final call or email requesting status, correct contact, or rebid timing
- Day 7: Classify as active, nurture, closed, no response, bad data, or do-not-contact

The client must approve the cadence. Newly sent proposals should not be treated like dormant records.

## Stage 6: Live Handoff

When interest appears:

- Do not negotiate price or binding terms
- Confirm the requested meeting or revision
- Notify the client immediately
- Send written notes
- Log handoff time
- Confirm the client acts promptly

## Stage 7: Reporting

Deliver a final package including:

- Record-level outcome dashboard
- Attempt history
- Reopened opportunities
- Revision requests
- Future rebid dates
- Closed-lost reasons
- Bad records
- Do-not-contact suppressions
- 30–90-day recommended actions
- Founder time by activity

## Client-Prospect Contact Script

Use a transparent identity in every contact:

> Hi, this is Clay with JanSan Sweep, following up on behalf of [Cleaning Company] regarding the cleaning proposal for [facility] sent around [date]. I'm helping them close the loop on older proposals. Is that project still under consideration, did you select another provider, or would a revised conversation be useful?

### If Open

> What needs to happen next, and who should [client owner] speak with?

### If Another Vendor Won

> Understood. When do you normally review that service again? I can record the appropriate month so [client] doesn't keep chasing you now.

### If Price Was the Issue

> Thank you—that's useful. I can't change their pricing, but I can ask whether a scope or frequency revision makes sense. Would you be open to that conversation?

### If Not Interested

> Understood. I'll record that and close the follow-up. Thank you for the clear answer.

Never imply Clay is the client's employee. The client must authorize the agency relationship and wording before any contact is made. See `06-COMPLIANCE-AND-DATA.md` for full disclosure and data-handling rules.
