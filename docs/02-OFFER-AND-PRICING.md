# JanSan Sweep — Offer and Pricing

## JanSan Sweep Recovery Sprint (Founding Offer)

**Price:** $199 prepaid
**Availability:** First three clients only
**Duration:** Seven business days after complete intake and approval
**Scope:** Up to 20 qualified dormant commercial proposals
**Deliverable:** Approved follow-up work, immediate handoffs, record-level notes, outcomes, and final dashboard
**Not guaranteed:** Replies, meetings, revisions, contract awards, revenue, or ROI

### Included

- Fifteen-minute kickoff
- Import and normalization of up to 20 records
- Eligibility and data-quality review
- Duplicate removal
- Opportunity prioritization
- Client-approved contact wording and cadence
- Manual calls and personalized emails
- Outcome and attempt tracking
- Immediate notification of sales interest
- Final status dashboard
- Closeout call and next-action recommendations

### Excluded

- New cold-lead generation
- Contact-list purchasing
- Consumer outreach
- Automated dialing or prerecorded calls
- Proposal rewriting or repricing
- Negotiation of binding terms
- More than 20 records
- Continued nurture after the sprint
- Contacting restricted procurement officials

## Qualified Record Standard

A record is accepted when it concerns commercial cleaning; the prospect previously requested, received, or participated in the proposal process; the client has a legitimate business basis for follow-up; the record is not subject to a communication blackout; it includes business, contact/role, phone or email, proposal date, facility/scope, and last-known status; and the client authorizes JanSan Sweep to make the specified contact.

## Recovered Opportunity Definition

A record is "recovered" only if JanSan Sweep obtains a substantive new status or next step: a review meeting requested, scope/pricing revision requested, active decision date confirmed, future rebid or renewal date identified, correct decision-maker provided, or another meaningful, documented next action.

Do not count email opens, voicemail drops, or generic acknowledgements as recovered.

## Outcome Codes

| Code | Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| RR | Review requested | Prospect agrees to a call or meeting |
| RV | Revision requested | Scope, price, frequency, or timing should be reconsidered |
| OP | Open pending | Still active with a confirmed next date |
| RB | Rebid date | Future renewal or bidding month identified |
| WI | Won independently | Client already won; record needed correction |
| LI | Lost—incumbent/competitor | Another provider retained or selected |
| LP | Lost—price | Price identified as primary issue |
| LT | Lost—timing/budget | Project delayed or budget unavailable |
| NR | No response | Approved sequence completed without contact |
| BD | Bad data | Contact details invalid or wrong person |
| DNC | Do not contact | Recipient requests no further outreach |
| X | Ineligible | Record does not satisfy sprint requirements |

## Pricing Roadmap

| Stage | Offer | Test Price | Trigger |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Founding | Up to 20 records | $199 prepaid | First three clients |
| Standard | Up to 20 records | $299 prepaid | Delivery process is reliable |
| Expanded | Up to 50 records | $499–$750 | Measured effort supports margin |
| JanSan Sweep Desk | Ongoing new and dormant follow-up | $500–$1,250/month | Client requests continuity |
| Hybrid | Base fee plus defined success fee | Custom | Attribution rules are proven |

These prices are tests, not market benchmarks. Avoid pure commission initially — a contract award depends on the client's pricing, capacity, revisions, and buyer process, none of which JanSan Sweep controls. JanSan Sweep must be paid for the defined work even when a prospect does not award a contract.

## Unit Economics

### Founding Sprint Assumptions

- Revenue: $199
- Maximum records: 20
- Target delivery time: 5–7 hours
- Direct cash cost: close to zero using existing tools
- Primary cost: Clay's time
- Target effective revenue before overhead and taxes: about $28–$40 per delivery hour

Track actual time for intake, cleanup, calls, email, notes, handoffs, and reporting. If a sprint repeatedly takes 10 hours, reduce scope, improve intake requirements, automate an internal bottleneck, or raise price.

## Refund Policy (Draft — Have Counsel Review)

- Full refund before intake work begins, less unavoidable processor fees if properly disclosed and permitted
- No change-of-mind refund after data work begins
- Refund if JanSan Sweep rejects the campaign because records are ineligible
- If fewer than 10 eligible records remain, allow substitutions, a smaller audit, or a pre-outreach refund
