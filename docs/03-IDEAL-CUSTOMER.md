# JanSan Sweep — Ideal Customer

## Ideal Customer Profile

| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| Business | Independent commercial janitorial or facility-cleaning company |
| Size | Approximately 5–50 employees; verify directly |
| Leadership | Owner/operator actively involved in sales |
| Revenue | Recurring commercial cleaning contracts |
| Sales motion | Walkthroughs and customized proposals |
| Volume | Ideally 5+ proposals monthly or 10+ dormant proposals |
| Sales staff | No dedicated salesperson consistently working every proposal |
| Records | Can provide proposal date, business contact, facility, scope, and notes |
| Capacity | Able to accept another account and respond quickly to live interest |
| Initial geography | Arizona and nearby time zones, then national expansion if validated |

## Best Subsegments

- Office janitorial firms
- Medical-office cleaners
- Commercial floor-care companies offering recurring service
- Post-construction cleaners pursuing maintenance contracts
- Regional cleaners serving property managers, churches, schools, dealerships, warehouses, and light industrial facilities

## Weak Fits

- Residential-only maid services
- Solo cleaners with minimal proposal volume
- National and large regional firms with dedicated sales departments
- Franchises with corporate-controlled sales processes
- Government-only bidders subject to formal communication restrictions
- Companies that cannot service new accounts
- Owners unwilling to prove contacts previously requested or received a quote

## Qualification Questions

1. Do you sell recurring commercial cleaning?
2. How many proposals did you send in the last 30, 90, and 180 days?
3. How many lack a clear yes, no, or decision date?
4. Did each prospect request a quote, invite a walkthrough, or otherwise engage?
5. Where are those proposals stored?
6. Who owns the second, third, and fourth follow-up?
7. Can you export the contact, facility, proposal date, scope, and notes?
8. Are any formal government or procurement bids with communication restrictions?
9. If an opportunity reopens, who handles the review call or revision?
10. Can your operation absorb another recurring account?

## Prospect Score

Score each dimension from 0–2:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial focus | Residential | Mixed | Primarily commercial |
| Owner access | Unknown | Owner named | Direct access likely |
| Size | Under 5 or over 50 | Uncertain | Estimated 5–50 |
| Proposal evidence | Unknown | Quotes mentioned | Walkthrough/custom bids emphasized |
| Sales gap | Dedicated sales team | Unknown | Owner-led/no salespeople |
| Capacity | Unclear | General growth | Explicitly pursuing accounts |

Call scores of 8–12 first.

## Disqualification Rules

Do not proceed to a paid pilot if any of the following are true:

- The company is residential-only with no commercial proposal history
- Fewer than 10 legitimate dormant proposals exist
- Records are purchased contacts or unrelated consumer leads
- The engagement would require contacting restricted government-procurement officials
- The owner cannot name a person to receive live-interest handoffs
- The owner wants robocalling, prerecorded messages, or mass unsolicited texting
