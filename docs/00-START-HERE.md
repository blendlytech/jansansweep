# JanSan Sweep — Project Index

This Claude Project supports **JanSan Sweep**, a founder-delivered, done-for-you proposal follow-up and bid-recovery service for owner-operated commercial cleaning companies. This file is the navigation map for every other document in the knowledge base. Read this first in any new conversation before relying on other files.

## What JanSan Sweep Is

JanSan Sweep follows up on legitimate, previously sent commercial-cleaning proposals that never received a clear decision. It turns ambiguous, dormant records into documented outcomes: review calls, revision requests, confirmed rebid dates, correct decision-makers, or clean closures.

It is **not** cold lead generation, purchased lists, mass automated outreach, an AI voice agent, debt collection, or a guarantee of signed contracts.

## Current Business Stage

Pre-revenue validation. Zero paid clients as of the last update. The immediate objective is to close and manually deliver the first $199 prepaid pilot without building software or spending money.

## Confirmed Decisions (Summary)

- Vertical: independent commercial janitorial/facility-cleaning companies
- Buyer: owner, founder, or sales-responsible operator
- Target size: approximately 5–50 employees
- Offer: JanSan Sweep Recovery Sprint, up to 20 qualified dormant proposals, 7 business days
- Founding price: $199 prepaid for the first three clients; $299 standard afterward
- Channel: founder-led manual cold calling
- Delivery: manual calls and personalized email, no automated outbound contact
- Product rule: revenue before software — no custom app until 3 paid pilots validate a repeated bottleneck

Full detail lives in `01-BUSINESS-PLAN.md` and `02-OFFER-AND-PRICING.md`.

## Unvalidated Assumptions

- Target owners possess at least 10 usable dormant proposals
- $199 is credible and low-risk enough to close quickly
- One founder can deliver 20 records in 7 hours or fewer
- Transparent third-party follow-up will be accepted by prospects
- At least one of the first three clients will want an ongoing retainer

Track these in `08-METRICS-AND-VALIDATION.md` until proven or disproven.

## Conflict Rule
